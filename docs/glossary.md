Standard business terms used in PeakFit procurement and finance reporting.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Supplier** | An external business PeakFit buys goods or services from. Also called a **vendor**. |
| **Centre** | A PeakFit fitness centre. Also called a **club**, **gym**, **cost centre** or **profit centre**. There are 23 centres across NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA and ACT. |
| **Financial year (FY)** | 1 July to 30 June. FY2026 = 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026. The latest complete FY as at 31 October 2026 is FY2026. |
| **H1 / H2** | First half (July–December) and second half (January–June) of a financial year. |
| **Spend** | The value of **Approved** purchase orders, excluding GST. Pending and Rejected purchase orders are never spend. |
| **Category spend** | Spend grouped by the **supplier's category** (e.g. Cleaning Services, HVAC Services). Spend codes are a separate, more granular coding and are not used for category reporting. |
| **Tail spend** | Spend with the long tail of suppliers: rank suppliers by approved spend for the period (highest first); the suppliers that together make up the first 80% of total spend are the **core** suppliers (including the supplier whose spend takes the running total past 80%). Every supplier after that is a **tail** supplier, and their combined spend is tail spend. |
| **Maverick spend** | Approved spend with a supplier that had **no contract in force on the PO date** (no contract of any status whose start and end dates cover the PO date). Also called off-contract spend. |
| **At-risk supplier** | Defined in the Supplier Management Process (PF-PROC-SMP-004, Section 4). |
| **Expiring soon** | Defined in the Contract Renewal Process (PF-PROC-CRP-002, Section 3). |
| **At-Risk Renewal Watchlist** | Monthly report of at-risk suppliers with contracts expiring soon (PF-PROC-CRP-002, Section 6). |
| **PO splitting** | Defined in the Procurement Approval Policy (PF-FIN-POL-011, Section 3). |
| **Material overrun** | Defined in the Budget Governance Standard (PF-FIN-STD-006, Section 5). |
| **Spend per FTE** | Approved spend divided by the average monthly full-time-equivalent staff for the same centres and period. Headcount and FTE are monthly snapshots and are never summed across months. |
| **Supplier Risk Score** | 0–100 score shown on the Supplier Scorecard in the PeakFit Procurement Insights app. Higher means riskier. The calculation is maintained in the app. |
